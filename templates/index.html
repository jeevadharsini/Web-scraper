        Good to know before you scrape
        • Only public pages are fetched. Logins, CAPTCHAs and anti-bot protections are never bypassed.
        • The site's robots.txt is checked first, and a page it disallows is skipped.
        • Pages that build their content with JavaScript may look empty here, because this tool reads only the HTML the server sends. Those pages need a browser-based approach.
        • Check a website's terms of use before scraping it, and keep your requests occasional.
        • Nothing is saved on the server. Results are kept in this page only until you clear or close it.
